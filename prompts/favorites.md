# Prompt log: favorites in the filter

Prompts are quoted as written, in Russian (the chat language), with an English summary.

## Prompt

> Давай звезды как избранное добавим в фильтр категорий

*Summary: let's add stars as favorites in the category filter.*

**Agent response (summary):** added a "Favorites" row at the top of the category list, with the same tick box. "Clear" clears it together with the categories, and the statistics apply it too.

**The agent's call, and I went along with it:** several categories mean "any of them", but favorites plus a category means "both": a favorite that also has this category. That seemed the more useful reading, and a short hint in the filter says so.
